## Welcome

Welcome to the Star-Pay API Documentation — your comprehensive guide to integrating and operating the Star-Pay payment gateway within your systems.

Star-Pay is a secure, scalable, API-first payment gateway designed to simplify digital transaction processing for modern applications. Our platform enables fast, reliable, and traceable payment experiences with built-in role-based authorization for accountability and operational efficiency.

Whether you're building customer-facing checkout systems, recurring billing solutions, or real-time payment verification features, Star-Pay provides the flexibility and security your team needs.

## Who This Guide Is For

This documentation is intended for:

- Developers integrating payment functionality
- Technical teams working on system integrations
- Product engineers implementing secure payment workflows
